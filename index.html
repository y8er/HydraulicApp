Segments in series at constant mass flow. Within a segment: isothermal momentum equation integrated with local Z, density, viscosity, and friction factor (64/Re below Re 2,000, Colebrook above 4,000, linear blend between); choke = local isothermal Mach 1 (real-gas dP/dρ). Between segments of different ID: isothermal real-gas Bernoulli across the junction plus a loss K on the small-bore velocity (Crane sudden-change K by default); the contraction root is bracketed above the residual minimum at Mach 1/√(1+K); "no solution" at a transition is a model limit, not a confirmed choke. Capacity by bisection on mass flow, controlling location from the limiting event on the infeasible side. Flow mode reports a limit only when the bracket closes against a verified event and the outlet-pressure residual is checked; otherwise "not resolved". The operating point is solved independently of capacity. A limit before the final segment is internal; downstream segments are then not resolved. Corrugated hose: multiplier on steel-pipe friction at the same ID (Penflex EB-111 basis). Sweet gas from SG (Standing, DAK, LGE), SCF at 60 °F, horizontal. Screening tool.
